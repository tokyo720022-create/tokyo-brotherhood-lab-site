A phone-friendly AniList companion that helps you choose what to watch or read based on the time you have, while bringing your lists, discovery tools, progress tracking, and anime/manga information together.

Project information

Choose what to watch or read next.

What's Next uses your AniList account to help with discovery, planning, and keeping track of the anime and manga you care about.

✦

What you can do

  • Pick by time: get suggestions for watching, reading, or either, based on the time you have.
  • Discover titles: explore anime and manga by genre, format, popularity, trends, and more.
  • Manage your lists: browse, filter, and update your AniList entries and progress.
  • Explore details: open media, character, staff, and studio pages with related recommendations.
  • Track your journey: view airing schedules, statistics, goals, and progress tools.
  • Useful on the go: use privacy options, cover blurring, and offline access to previously saved data.

Bugs & feedback

Found a bug? Let me know.

Bug reports and suggestions are handled on GitHub, so there is nothing to sign up for on this website (a free GitHub account is needed to post). The bug-report button opens a new issue with a ready-made template that asks for:

  • The app version or APK file name
  • Your device model and Android version
  • A description of the problem and the steps to reproduce it
  • What you expected to happen and what actually happened

Getting started

How to try What's Next.

Open the web app or choose an APK from the release list. Sign in with AniList to use your lists and personalised suggestions. Check the release notes before installing an Android build, and report bugs through GitHub.
